01

Getting Started

Understand what TGM Companion does, how its data is handled, and where to begin.

Introduction

Welcome to TGM Companion

TGM Companion is a community-made tool designed to help players plan Investments, Building Star-Ups, resource requirements, upgrade times, and other progression-related decisions.

This Wiki explains how the different systems work, how to use the tools on this website, and contains tips gathered from player experience and verified game data.

Unofficial projectTGM Companion is fan-made and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Grand Mafia or its developers.
Planner

Investments

The Investment Planner lets you browse investment categories and individual investments. For supported investments, it can show:

  • Level and Original Time
  • Cash, Arms, Cargo, Metal, Diamonds, and Oil
  • Crypto Coins, Family Currency, and Family Insignia
  • Gold and Influence
  • Investment prerequisites

You can also keep track of completed investment levels. Newer categories may remain Under Construction while data is collected and verified.

Planner

Building Star-Ups

The Star-Up Planner helps you record the Star-Up progress of your buildings and calculate what is still required to reach a selected target.

Your current building progress and target are stored locally in your browser so you can continue planning later.

Time

Original Time

Original Time is the base investment time shown by the game before your personal bonuses are applied.

Example3D 03:46:08

When submitting investment data, always provide the original game time rather than a reduced time from your own account.

Time

Time with buffs

Your actual investment time may be lower because of bonuses such as Investment Speed, VIP bonuses, Family Help, or other active bonuses.

TGM Companion keeps the Original Time separate from player-adjusted calculations so the underlying source data stays consistent for everyone.

Requirements

Prerequisites

Some investments require another investment to reach a certain level first.

Hijack Boost Lv. 1requiresBasic Resource Lv. 1

TGM Companion only lists prerequisites that have been confirmed. Missing requirements are not guessed.

Data quality

Complete, partial, and unknown data

Data complete
All levels and required data currently expected for that investment have been collected.
Partial data
Some levels or values are known, but more information is still missing.
Under construction
There is not enough verified information to treat the investment or category as complete.

Unknown values are deliberately shown as Unknown instead of being estimated.

Privacy

Saving your progress

TGM Companion stores planner progress locally in your browser. This includes Investment progress, Building Star-Up progress, presets, planner settings, and theme settings.

Your planner progress is not automatically uploaded to a server. Clearing browser data can remove locally saved progress.

Backup

Back up your data

The Dashboard includes Backup all and Restore all. A backup is recommended before:

  • Clearing browser data
  • Switching browser, computer, or phone
  • Reinstalling the PWA
  • Making major changes to your saved planner progress
Community

Community data

Players can submit missing Investment or Star-Up information through Submit data in the footer.

Submissions are reviewed before being added to the main dataset. Screenshots and independent submissions help compare and verify values.

Wiki

Game data vs. tips

Game data includes factual values such as resource costs, upgrade times, levels, prerequisites, and Influence.

Tips and strategies are recommendations based on player experience. They can help with decisions, but a strategy that works for one account may not be ideal for another.

02

Tips & Tricks

Practical player guidance for using resources and time more efficiently.

Strategy, not a rulebook

The advice below is meant as guidance. There is not always one correct way to progress, and the best choice can depend on your account, Family, events, and playstyle.

Planning

Plan before spending

Before starting an expensive Investment or Building Star-Up, check what comes after it. One upgrade may be affordable while the next prerequisite requires significantly more resources.

Looking several steps ahead can prevent getting stuck halfway through a progression path.

Resources

Don't look at one resource only

An upgrade may look affordable because you have enough Cash, while Arms, Cargo, Metal, Diamonds, or another resource is actually the bottleneck.

Compare all required resources and identify which one is lowest relative to your upcoming requirements.

Requirements

Check prerequisites first

Before saving for a high-level investment, confirm whether another investment must be completed first.

Knowing the dependency path in advance prevents saving everything for an investment you cannot unlock yet.

Time

Keep Original Time separate

Original Time is the unmodified game value. Your personal time can differ because of Investment Speed, VIP, Family Help, and other bonuses.

Use Original Time when comparing or submitting source data so different accounts can be compared consistently.

Time

Use longer investments around offline time

Longer investments are often convenient before sleeping, work, school, or another period away from the game. Shorter upgrades are easier to handle while you are active and able to start the next one quickly.

The aim is to reduce unnecessary idle time between completed upgrades.

Resources

Don't spend everything at once

Completing one large upgrade while reducing every resource to nearly zero can make the next step much harder.

Look at the following requirements and consider keeping a reserve, especially for resources that are difficult for your account to replace quickly.

Star-Ups

Be careful with rare currencies

Family Currency and Family Insignia can become important progression bottlenecks. Check upcoming Building Star-Up requirements before committing large amounts elsewhere.

The Star-Up Planner can help you see what your selected target still requires.

Planning

Include prerequisites in the real cost

If your target requires another investment first, include that prerequisite in the full cost of reaching your target rather than treating it as a separate surprise.

Target Investment→Required Investment→Required level
Data quality

0 and Unknown are not the same

0The resource has been confirmed as not required for that level.
UnknownThe value has not been sufficiently verified yet.

Never treat an unknown value as zero when planning resources.

Investments

Look beyond the next level

For investments with several known levels, browse higher levels before committing. This shows how quickly resource costs and Original Time increase.

It can help decide whether to push the same investment further or use resources elsewhere first.

Planner

Keep your progress updated

The planners become more useful when saved progress matches your actual account.

After completing several Investments or Star-Ups, update TGM Companion so later planning does not include work you have already finished.

Backup

Make backups regularly

Create a Backup all file after substantial progress and before clearing browser data, switching devices or browsers, or reinstalling the PWA.

Community

Verify community data

One community submission should not automatically be treated as correct. Where possible, compare values against screenshots or independent submissions from multiple players.

This is especially useful for resource amounts, Original Time, Gold, Influence, prerequisites, and maximum levels.

Community

Submit corrections instead of working around errors

If TGM Companion does not match the game, submit the correct information rather than adjusting around the error only for yourself.

A useful submission includes the exact Investment or Building, its level, the values shown by the game, and preferably screenshot evidence.
